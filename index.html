Denim · Research · Innovation

Built around denim.

A research-minded industry professional with 7 years of experience in denim fabric development, finishing and process optimization.

I currently serve as Deputy Manager – Fabric Finishing & Development at Salek Textile Limited, Bangladesh. My technical work includes overdyeing, bleaching, mercerizing, sanforizing, coating, shade matching, bulk consistency and process optimization.

My academic interests focus on sustainability-related technology and practice adoption in textile and apparel manufacturing, particularly denim manufacturing.

I am interested in sustainable textile production, textile wet processing, water and chemical consumption reduction, recycled materials, resource efficiency, and the relationship between sustainability practices, cost efficiency and competitive advantage.
