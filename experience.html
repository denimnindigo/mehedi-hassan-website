02 · What changed

From observing machines to making process decisions.

The early stage was about learning: seeing how dyeing, weaving, finishing, laboratory testing and utilities work together.

Production work then added responsibility for trials, planning, automation support and traceability. Fabric development and finishing brought a stronger focus on technical problem-solving, buyer requirements and reproducibility.

Managerial responsibility now brings these layers together — technical execution, development, efficiency, quality, sustainability and collaboration.

The thread through every role

Understand the process. Improve the process. Create value from the improvement.

That progression is what connects my industrial experience with my interest in sustainability and technology adoption in textile manufacturing.
